## The r/TikTokLive Mystery

A common frustration pattern:

> "I go live, and BAM—50 people in the room. I start talking to them, and suddenly they all leave. 10 minutes later, I'm talking to myself. Did I get shadowbanned?"

You didn't get shadowbanned. You got **benchmarked**.

## The Normal Decay Curve

TikTok Live gives almost every new stream a small "test injection" of traffic (200–500 exposures) in the first 10 minutes. This is the **Honeymoon Phase**.

### Phase 1: The Push (0–10 Mins)

- **Source:** Exploratory FYP traffic.
- **Goal:** TikTok is checking your "Click-to-Watch" rate.
- **Your Job:** Look interesting in the thumbnail/preview.

### Phase 2: The Cliff (10–20 Mins)

- **Source:** Traffic stops. Now you run on retained viewers.
- **Goal:** TikTok checks "Average Watch Time."
- **Your Job:** Keep those 50 people from leaving.

### Phase 3: The Flatline (20+ Mins)

- **Source:** None (unless you passed Phase 2).
- **Result:** If you failed retention, you stay at 0–5 viewers until you restart.

## Why Viewers Leave at the Cliff

The mistake most streamers make is **relaxing** after the intro.

1.  **Minute 0:** High energy intro! "Welcome in!"
2.  **Minute 5:** Settling into gameplay/content.
3.  **Minute 10:** Silence, focus, or low-energy chatting.

The viewers who arrived at Minute 8 didn't see your intro. They walked into a quiet room. **They leave.**

## The Solution: The 10-Minute Reset

You must treat your stream like a loop, not a line. Every 10 minutes, you need to "Reset the Room."

### How to Reset

At Minute 9, Minute 19, Minute 29:

1.  **Stop what you are doing.**
2.  **Re-state the goal:** "If you're just joining, we are trying to win 5 games in a row. We are currently at 2 wins."
3.  **Call to Action:** "Tap the screen if you think we lose this next one."
4.  **Restart the energy:** Act like the stream just began.

## Conclusion

The "Early Push" is a loan. Retention is how you pay it back.

If your stream dies after 15 minutes, it means you didn't convert the free traffic into paid traffic (watch time). **Reset your room**, and you might survive the cliff.
